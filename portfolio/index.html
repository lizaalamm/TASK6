01 — About

I care about how a product feels and how it holds up.

I'm a software engineer who works across the whole stack. Most of my time goes into making things that are simple to use on the surface and genuinely well-structured underneath — typed boundaries, predictable data flow, and no shortcuts that turn into bugs six weeks later.

Over two remote internships — first as a Software Engineer at Carbon Repro, then as a Full-Stack Developer Intern at U Devs — I worked the way professional teams work: feature branches, reviewed pull requests, protected main, shared staging environments, and code that someone else has to maintain.

My own builds lean ambitious. NeuralHub is a multi-agent healthcare platform with a full document-ingestion RAG pipeline, per-agent vector indexes and a hard line between client and API. Cyron is an offline-capable security operations platform that profiles users for 30 days, scores risk across five weighted factors, and isolates a compromised endpoint in under 30 seconds.

I like the intersection of the three: interface design that respects users, backend architecture that scales calmly, and intelligence that is explainable rather than a black box.

  • Ship in small, reviewable increments
  • Type the boundaries, not just the internals
  • If it isn't measurable, it isn't finished
  • Document it like the next dev is you in 3 months
